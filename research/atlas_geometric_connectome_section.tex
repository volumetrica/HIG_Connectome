\section{Atlas-constrained HIG and geometric correlation scales}
Let $C\in[-1,1]^{n\times n}$ denote a symmetric functional-connectivity
matrix with a fixed ROI order and $N\in\{0,1\}^{n\times n}$ its
anatomical parcel-contact adjacency in the same order.
For off-diagonal graph distance $d_N(i,j)$ define
\[
 K_k(i,j)=\mathbf{1}\{d_N(i,j)=k\},\qquad
 K_{\le k}(i,j)=\mathbf{1}\{i\ne j,\ d_N(i,j)\le k\}.
\]
The geometrically constrained functional matrices are
\[
 C_k^g=K_k\odot C,\qquad
 C_{\le k}^g=K_{\le k}\odot C,\qquad
 E_k=\sum_{\substack{i<j\\d_N(i,j)=k}} C_{ij}^2.
\]
The masks $K_k$ are disjoint on off-diagonal pairs and
$\sum_k E_k=\sum_{i<j,d_N(i,j)<\infty}C_{ij}^2$.
The nonnegative integer $(N^k)_{ij}$ counts walks, whereas
$K_k$ records \emph{shortest-path distance}. These are
different scales.

If $UNV=D$ is a Smith normal form over $\mathbb Z$, then
$G_{\mathbb Q}=V D_{\mathbb Q}^+ U$ satisfies
$NG_{\mathbb Q}N=N$ and $G_{\mathbb Q}NG_{\mathbb Q}=G_{\mathbb Q}$,
but is in general rational and is not necessarily Moore--Penrose.
An integral $G$ with $NGN=N$ exists precisely when all
nonzero invariant factors of $N$ are units. In particular, the
Smith form of a real-valued $C_k^g$ cannot be computed over
$\mathbb Z$ without a specified integer quantization.
Proposed $s^2$-model scale maps need independent verification of
their retained axioms.

The HIG of an ROI-indexed correlation image captures incidences
of its \emph{chosen matrix layout}; its image homology must not be
identified with the homology of the anatomical graph or abstract
functional network. An anatomical constraint on matrix entries
does not remove this representation distinction.

This is a proposed extension, not a newly validated experimental
or clinical claim. See the associated protocol document.
